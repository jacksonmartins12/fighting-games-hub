Replay Review

A short sheet to fill in after a session, with one replay open. It turns the ideas from Thinking in Bets into five questions: judge the decision instead of the result, separate skill from luck, write reads as odds and leave with one thing to practice.

How to use it. Pick one match, a loss or a win. You don't need to answer everything: one honest answer is worth more than five rushed ones. Saved reviews stay below so you can compare them week to week.
