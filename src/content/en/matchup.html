Matchup Tool

SF6 moduleChecked against Street Fighter 6 in October 2026. Balance patches can change numbers and systems; when they do, the fundamentals in The Path still apply.

Pick your fighter and the opponent. The tool crosses the two guides and the frame data already in this hub: what you can punish on block and with what, what you have to respect, each side's anti-airs and main tools, and the counterplay written in both guides.

How to use it. Read it before a set or after a loss, pick one or two things to try and take them to training mode. Each matchup has its own link, so you can save it or send it to a training partner.
