30-Day Plan

One short session a day for a month, in the order that makes sense: foundations, tools, neutral, then decisions and mindset. Each day tells you what to read in this hub and what to do with the controller. Most days take 20 to 40 minutes.

How to use it. Tick a day when you finish it; your progress is saved. Missing a day is fine: continue from where you stopped instead of trying to catch up. Days 7, 14 and 21 are review days, so the plan also fills your tracker and your replay reviews.
