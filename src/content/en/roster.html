Every fighter released in Street Fighter 6 as of October 2026, from the base game through the latest Year 4 DLC. Each guide covers how the character wins, their key special moves and normals with frame data, anti-airs, starter combos, practical tips and how to play against them. Filter by archetype or search by name, then open a fighter.

About the numbers. The key normals in each guide come from Capcom's official frame data pages (Classic controls), read in October 2026 after the August 2026 balance patch, and were rechecked against the Frame Assistant Tool (FAT) open data of October 22, 2026. Arjun, the newest fighter, uses FAT data only. The full frame tables and the Punish Finder use the FAT data. Combos come from community guides listed at the bottom of each fighter. Patches change values, so confirm anything important with the in-game frame meter. A dash (—) marks a value we couldn't confirm.

Notation: numpad directions for a character facing right — 2 = down, 6 = forward, 4 = back, 236 = quarter-circle forward, 623 = dragon punch motion, 214 = quarter-circle back, [4]6 = hold back then forward. L/M/H = light/medium/heavy, P/K = punch/kick. DR = Drive Rush, OD = Overdrive version, SA1–SA3 = Super Arts. In combos, a space between moves means a link or chain and > means a cancel. Classic notation (c.MK, QCF+P) is listed alongside.
