Character Guides

SF6 moduleChecked against Street Fighter 6 in October 2026. Balance patches can change numbers and systems; when they do, the fundamentals in The Path still apply.

Every fighter released in Street Fighter 6 as of October 2026, from the base game through the latest Year 4 DLC. Each guide covers how the character wins, their key special moves and normals with frame data, anti-airs, starter combos, practical tips and how to play against them, plus the full frame data and an "Oki and pressure, calculated" section: meaties after each knockdown, block strings and frame traps, all computed from the frame data. That section checks timing, not range: confirm each setup in training mode before using it (the warning at the top of the section explains why). Most guides also carry "Combo and oki routes from SF6 Lab": Iori's essential routes, used with his permission. Filter by archetype or search by name, then open a fighter.

About the numbers. The key normals in each guide come from Capcom's official frame data pages (Classic controls), read in October 2026 after the August 2026 balance patch, and were rechecked against the Frame Assistant Tool (FAT) open data of October 22, 2026. Arjun's guide was first written from FAT's data, when he was new; its key normals were later checked against Capcom's page of November 11, 2026, and match it. The full frame tables and the Punish Finder use the FAT data, checked against Capcom's pages of November 11, 2026: where the two differ the tables use Capcom's value, and moves only Capcom lists were added with the tag "from Capcom". Combos come from community guides listed at the bottom of each fighter. Patches change values, so confirm anything important with the in-game frame meter. A dash (—) marks a value we couldn't confirm.

Notation: numpad directions for a character facing right — 2 = down, 6 = forward, 4 = back, 236 = quarter-circle forward, 623 = dragon punch motion, 214 = quarter-circle back, [4]6 = hold back then forward. L/M/H = light/medium/heavy, P/K = punch/kick. DR = Drive Rush, OD = Overdrive version, SA1–SA3 = Super Arts. In combos, a space between moves means a link or chain and > means a cancel. Classic notation (c.MK, QCF+P) is listed alongside.
