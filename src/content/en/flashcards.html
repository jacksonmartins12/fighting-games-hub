Punish Flashcards

SF6 moduleChecked against Street Fighter 6 in October 2026. Balance patches can change numbers and systems; when they do, the fundamentals in The Path still apply.

Each card shows a move you just blocked. Answer in your head (or with the controller in training mode), then check. Cards you miss come back often; cards you know show up less and less.

How it works. Every card sits in one of three boxes. "Knew it" moves it one box up; "Didn't know" sends it back to box 1. Ten minutes a day against the opponent you meet most is enough to stop missing punishes in real matches.
