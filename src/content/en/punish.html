Punish Finder

SF6 moduleChecked against Street Fighter 6 in October 2026. Balance patches can change numbers and systems; when they do, the fundamentals in The Path still apply.

Pick your character and your opponent. The finder lists every move of theirs that is unsafe on block and, for each one, the moves of yours that are fast enough to punish it: your best normal, your best special that costs no meter and, if you want, the options that spend meter (OD moves and Super Arts).

How it works. If a move is −6 on block, you can act 6 frames before your opponent recovers. Any move of yours with 6 frames of startup or less hits them before they can block again. That's a guaranteed punish, as long as it reaches.

Before you trust a result. Range matters: many moves push you away when blocked, so a 4-frame jab that is fast enough may simply not reach. Test each punish in training mode by recording the dummy doing the move. The finder shows the starter; in a real punish you confirm it into your combo, and hitting during recovery is a Punish Counter (+4 frames and extra damage), which often lets heavier starters combo. When a move has several versions (spaced, charged), the finder uses the least punishable one.
